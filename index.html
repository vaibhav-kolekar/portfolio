CASE 01 · THG INGENUITY · GLOBAL COMMERCE

Making every online discount legally honest — across 100+ brands.

The EU Omnibus Directive (Article 6a) requires advertised discounts to be measured against the lowest price of the last 30 days, not the RRP. Getting it wrong means regulatory risk; getting it slow means broken storefronts for every brand on the platform.

My role

Co-designed and delivered the change across three layers of the stack — pricing data service, GraphQL API and storefront — and extended the offers service so promotions discount from the compliant base price.

Key design decisions

  • One field, not a query. Exposed the 30-day low as a single API field so no consumer ever scans price history.
  • Fail closed. If a prior price is missing, the savings claim is suppressed — never a misleading discount.
  • Audit-ready data. SKU-level price history with multi-year retention and a configurable rolling window.
  • Safe rollout. A per-site config flag let one market go live without touching any other brand.

Outcome

Compliance shipped market by market under CAB-approved change control, with 5 Omnibus price classifiers mapped through the offer lifecycle and zero impact on other brands.

CASE 02 · CANDESCENT · DIGITAL BANKING

Connecting core banking platforms — without a second of downtime.

Two enterprise banking applications — Transaction Gateway and Activate Enterprise — had to be integrated while real customer money kept flowing. In banking, downtime and lost messages are not an option.

My role

Led the integration in Java, Spring Boot and Kafka, and engineered a second secure, event-driven integration between Activate Enterprise and the Remote Teller platform.

Key design decisions

  • Event-driven, not point-to-point. Kafka decoupled the systems so each could scale and fail independently.
  • Planned cutover. Migration designed so traffic switched over with zero downtime.
  • Security as a gate. SonarQube, Mend and Coverity findings remediated to meet bank deployment standards.

Outcome

5,000+ financial transactions per day moved between systems with zero downtime at cutover, and the Remote Teller integration sustaining 10,000+ calls per day at peak.
